GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Does a proxy make me completely anonymous?

No. A proxy changes how your traffic is routed, but it does not guarantee anonymity. Websites may still identify you through accounts, cookies, browser fingerprints, or other signals.

Is every proxy service safe?

No. Research providers carefully. Free services may have limits or collect data. Never enter sensitive information into a service you do not trust.

Can I add my own services and links?

Yes. Edit the HTML and CSS files in this project to add verified services, a contact page, terms, and your own branding.
